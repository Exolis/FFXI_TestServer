    1. Campaign Areas (Region Info / Map System):
        a. Resources: Numerical value per area representing available temporary items. Displayed on Campaign map when hovering over area points.
        b. Fortifications: Numerical value representing an area's defenses. Influenced by number of soldiers participating in that area's battles. Each area has a fort with 4 targetable points (sides of fort structure or separate points). Dominant side holds the fort. Offensive battles = allies attack beastmen-held forts. Defensive battles = allies defend their own forts.
        c. Influence: Graphical bars showing each of the 3 allied nations' influence AND the Beastman Confederate's influence over an area. Tallied weekly (every Monday after Japan midnight, similar to Conquest). The side with most gains controls the area for that week. More areas controlled = stronger benefits for troops.
        d. Reconnaissance: Numerical level for each stronghold. Higher recon = more info the battle conditions NPC will provide to players.
        e. Morale: Graphical bar per stronghold. Higher morale = more formidable troops become on the battlefield.
        f. Prosperity: Graphical bar per stronghold. Higher prosperity = more Campaign funds generated, and higher rate of technological/industrial means for Campaign Ops.

    2. War Conditions (viewable via "Campaign" menu under Region Info):
        a. National Influence: Shows which army has most influence in each region. Determines who controls the area each week.
        b. Battle Strategies: Each nation (San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst) has unique military policy. Determined by previous week's tally results. Strategies change based on battle results, area influence, and player actions/assessments.
        c. Internal Policies: Logistics/reinforcement policies per nation. Also determined weekly and influenced by Tactical Assessment polling.
        Note: There may be a time discrepancy of several hours between actual current war conditions and those displayed in the menu.

    3. Tactical Assessment:
        a. Leader: Players assess the views of a nation's leaders and side with those of their choice.
        b. Battle Strategies: Players assess the battle strategies of a nation's forces.
        c. Internal Policies: Players assess the internal policies of a nation's forces.
        General: Conducted by speaking to the appropriate NPC (curate in San d'Oria, supply commander in Bastok, morale manager in Windurst). Affiliated players may participate once per week. Results are tallied in Campaign totals and influence the following week's policy decisions (taking into account the nation's elite opinions as well).

    4. Campaign Battles:
        a. Joining a Battle:
            - Player travels to area where Campaign battle is underway (teleport via city Arbiters, walking, Retrace, Recall teleports, Chocobo rental, or Cavernous Maws). Must have previously zoned into the area for teleport to be available.
            - Teleport Arbiters: Scarlette C.A. (H-9) Southern San d'Oria (S), Narkissa C.A. (E-8) Bastok Markets (S), Wenonah C.A. (G-10) Windurst Waters (S).
            - Arbiters will NOT teleport you to dungeons (Eldieme, Crawlers' Nest, Garlaige).
        b. Allied Tags:
            - Issued by Campaign Arbiter NPC (on-field arbiters in battle zones). Wartime sword icon appears before player name.
            - While tagged: defeating Campaign monsters earns Allied Notes. No EXP loss on death. No EXP/items/skillups from kills directly (rewards come via assessment).
            - Tags are LOST if player changes areas (including Tractor, Warp to Home Point within zone).
            - Tags can only be acquired when the wartime icon is displayed before the Arbiter's name.
        c. Performance Assessment:
            - Talk to Arbiter and choose "Performance assessment" to turn in tags and claim EXP/Allied Notes.
            - Automatic assessment occurs when Campaign Battle ends.
            - Rewards based on: healing/enhancing other players or NPCs under Campaign status, AND damage dealt to enemy beastmen.
            - Rewards are individually recorded; party/alliance not required; partial kills count.
            - Starting cap per Allied Tag: 600 EXP / 300 Allied Notes (ratio is 50% of EXP earned). Cap increases with medal rank.
        d. Allied Union:
            - Optional registration via Campaign Arbiter. If player contributions exceed threshold, a coffer spawns for the Union.
            - All registered Union members can lot on spoils via treasure pool.
            - Unregistered players' efforts get randomly assigned to a Union but they cannot open/lot.
        e. Offensive vs Defensive:
            - Area owned by nation = defensive battle (solo-friendly, fight alongside NPC allies).
            - Area overrun by beastmen = offensive battle (teamwork recommended, raid enemy stronghold, plan attacks, form teams).
        f. Misc Rules:
            - No EXP loss on death while tagged.
            - No limit on number of battles per day or total EXP/AN earned daily.
            - Players can engage Campaign enemies regardless of party/alliance orientation.
            - If beastmen control an area adjacent to an allied city, they will sack the city. If allies hold a zone adjacent to a beastmen stronghold, it becomes subject to Campaign battles.

    5. Campaign Ops (Special Operations):
        General: Requires 1 "Op Credit" per operation. Credits refresh once per Vana'diel day (max 7 stored). With "Rhapsody in Mauve" key item, credits refresh every 10 Earth minutes (max 7 unchanged). Players under lvl 10 cannot gain EXP but can still earn Allied Notes. Ops availability depends on: medal rank, regional control, repeated clears of lower-tier ops, and nation's condition/strategies. EXP awarded on objective completion; Allied Notes awarded after speaking to Campaign Ops NPC.

        Campaign Ops NPCs: Rasdinice (I-9) Southern San d'Oria (S), Hieronymus (E-8) Bastok Markets (S), Emhi Tchaoryo (H-9) Windurst Waters (S).

        a. Resource Procurement (Stock and Awe I-IV, Materiel Storm I-IV, Search and Seizure):
            - Player delivers needed supplies to their nation.
            - Higher tiers unlock at higher medal ranks.
            - Meet at Quartermaster NPC: (H-9) San d'Oria (S), (H-9) Bastok Markets (S), (F-9) Windurst Waters (S).
        b. Supply Transport (Vanguard I-IV, Crimson Domino I-IV, Bridge Too Far):
            - Escort reinforcements to outposts, bring supplies to strongholds, or devise safe supply transport plans.
        c. Security (Streetsweeper I-III, Delta Strike I-III, Steel Resolve I-IV, Magna Cache I-III, Hazardous Materials):
            - Find suspicious objects in cities, patrol territory to eliminate threats, fortify strongholds against siege turrets, expand provision storehouses.
        d. Supply Manufacture (Crystal Fist I-IV, Iron Anvil I-IV):
            - Lend crafting knowledge to unskilled crafters / crafting adepts.
            - Meet at Adjutant NPC: (D-8) San d'Oria (S), (J-9) Bastok Markets (S), (F-9) Windurst Waters (S).
        e. Offensive Operations (Smokescreen I-V, Pit Spider I-III, By Light of Fire, Splitting Heirs/Cracking Shells/Plucking Wings, Kinslayer, Fiat Lux):
            - Assist in siege of enemy strongholds, ambush enemy supply caravans, obliterate beastmen campgrounds in instanced areas (Everbloom Hollow / Ruhotz Silvermines / Ghoyu's Reverie).
            - Major beastman ops: Splitting Heirs (S vs Orcs in La Vaule), Cracking Shells (B vs Quadav in Beadeaux), Plucking Wings (W vs Yagudo in Castle Oztroja).
            - Fiat Lux: Defeat The Shadow Lord in Throne Room (S). Requires Medal of Altana.
            - Kinslayer: Defeat Shadowguard Demons in Castle Zvahl (S).
        f. Defensive Operations (Aegis Scream I-V, Granite Rose I-III):
            - Fend off beastman siege against nation strongholds.
            - Aid soldiers defending instanced areas (Everbloom Hollow / Ruhotz Silvermines / Ghoyu's Reverie).
        g. Intel Gathering (Hawk Eye I-IV, Deep Cover I-III, Prying Eyes I-II, Slaughterhouse I-IV, Bailey Borer, Frozen Flame):
            - Gather intelligence from enemy fortresses, surveillance of enemy equipment, infiltrate enemy strongholds for intel on weapon stores.
            - Slaughterhouse: Destroy key locations in enemy fortress.
            - Bailey Borer: Destroy the Zvahl Fortalice in Zvahl (S) zones. Requires Wings of Honor rank.
        h. Military Training (Brave Dawn I-III, Cut and Cauterize I-III):
            - Assist in training new recruits (combat training).
            - Assist in medical training of recruits.

        Instanced Ops Notes:
            - Some ops use instanced areas entered via Allied Ensign, Beastman Ensign, or Fortilace points in Shadowreign areas.
            - Party of up to 6; leader must have op activated.
            - Exit via Dawn Aureola or Twilight Aureola after completion. Leaving early = failure.
            - Non-participants can still enter, assist, and receive EXP.

    6. Campaign Medals:
        a. Evaluation System:
            - Evaluation possible every 120 hours (5 Earth days) after enlistment, transfer, or last evaluation.
            - With "Rhapsody in Mauve" key item: evaluation period reduced to 1 Earth hour.
            - Evaluation Officers: Alfred (K-10) Southern San d'Oria (S), Annabelle (F-8) Bastok Markets (S), Llewellyn (G-9) Windurst Waters (S).
            - Judgments: Promotion (rank up, eval reset), Status Quo (rank stays, eval reset), Demotion (rank down, eval halved).
            - Demotion can be prevented by progress in Wings of the Goddess nation quest branch.
        b. Rank Ups/Down Ranks (in order):
            - Ribbons: Bronze Ribbon of Service §, Brass Ribbon of Service §§, Allied Ribbon of Bravery §§§, Allied Ribbon of Glory §§§§
            - Stars: Bronze Star §, Sterling Star §§, Mythril Star §§§, Golden Star §§§§
            - Emblems: Copper Emblem of Service §, Iron Emblem of Service §§, Steelknight Emblem §§§, Holyknight Emblem §§§§
            - Wings: Brass Wings of Service §, Mythril Wings of Service §§, Wings of Integrity §§§, Wings of Honor §§§§
            - Medals: Starlight Medal §, Moonlight Medal §§, Dawnlight Medal §§§, Medal of Altana §§§§
        c. Evaluation Criteria:
            - Individual conduct in Campaign battle
            - Campaign Ops achievements (including assisting other PCs)
            - Accruing Allied Notes through normal Campaign Ops
            - Successful Chocobo Digging of Allied Notes in Shadowreign areas
        d. Bonuses of Medals:
            - Higher-level Campaign Ops become available.
            - Player's voice carries more weight in Tactical Assessments.
            - Allied Tag EXP/AN capacity increases with rank.
            - Sigil duration increases with each medal (see Sigil section).
            - Wings of the Goddess nation quest progress guarantees a minimum rank floor (cannot be demoted below it).
        e. Medal Validity:
            - Each medal is valid for 30 Earth days. After expiration, Campaign services dependent on that medal become inaccessible.
            - Renewal via Campaign Evaluation Officer.
        f. Allegiance Change Penalty:
            - Rejoining a previously left nation costs 300 Allied Notes and penalizes with loss of 2 medal ranks.
            - Progress toward medal and evaluation period reset upon changing allegiance.

    7. Sigil:
        - Buff similar to Signet/Sanction/Ionis, effective only in Wings of the Goddess areas (including Odyssey and Walk of Echoes).
        - Granted by Allied Officers: Miliart T.K. (L-9) Southern San d'Oria (S), Millard I.M. (G-5) Bastok Markets (S), Mindala-Andola C.C. (G-5) Windurst Waters (S).
        - Requires a valid Campaign Medal to receive.
        - Base effects: Obtain crystals from enemies in nation-controlled areas. No TP loss while resting. Increased HP recovery while resting. Increased defense/evasion vs claimed target (Even Match or weaker).
        - Optional bonuses (50 Allied Notes each): Latent Regen, Latent Refresh, Increased Meal Duration.
        - Bonus option: Reduced EXP loss on KO (if Freelance NPC Titania is allied to your nation).
        - Duration: Base 3h15m. Each medal rank adds 15 minutes. At Medal of Altana = 8 hours total.
        - Regen/Refresh/Food Duration scale with number of areas nation controls (0 areas = 45% HP cap / 35% MP cap / +20% food; 11 areas = 95% HP / 85% MP / +240% food).
        - Sigil, Sanction, and Signet are mutually exclusive (only one at a time).
        - Player can have both Sigil and Allied Tags simultaneously.

    8. Allied Notes:
        - Campaign currency similar to Conquest Points / Imperial Standing.
        - Earned from: Campaign Battle performance assessments, Campaign Ops completion, Chocobo Digging in Shadowreign areas.
        - Spent on: Equipment, services, miscellaneous items from Campaign Officials (nation-specific gear shops).
        - Can purchase from foreign nations at increased rates.
        - Vendors per nation:
            - San d'Oria (Knights of the Iron Ram): Miliart T.K. - Gear includes I.R. Jack Coat, Iron Ram Shield, full armor sets, weapons (Iron Ram Horn/Lance/Pick), accessories (Patronus Ring, Fox Earring, etc.), Iron Ram Hauberk (75k), Griffinclaw/R.K. Sigil Ring (100k/50k at Medal of Altana).
            - Bastok (Republican Legion's Fourth Division): Millard I.M. - Gear includes Fourth Tunica, Fourth Gun, full armor sets, weapons (Fourth Toporok/Mace/Zaghnal), accessories (Shield Collar, Bull Necklace, etc.), Fourth Brunne (75k), Lex Talionis/Fourth Mantle (100k/50k at Medal of Altana).
            - Windurst (The Cobras): Mindala-Andola C.C. - Gear includes Cobra Tunica, Cobra Claymore, full armor sets, weapons (Cobra Baghnakhs/Knife/Bow), accessories (Capricornian Rope, Cougar Pendant, etc.), Cobra Harness/Robe (75k), Samudra/Mrc.Mjr. Charm (100k/50k at Medal of Altana).
        - Common items (all nations): Sprinter's Shoes (980), Scroll of Instant Retrace (10), Pilgrim Tunica (1000), Recall Rings (5000 each - Jugner/Pashhow/Meriphataud), Cells (3000 each - Cobalt/Rubicund/Xanthous/Jade).
        - Allied Ring: 15,000 notes (requires your nation to control Throne Room (S)).
        - Livery gear: 10,000 notes (Royal Guard / Mythril Musketeer / Patriarch Protector).
        - Also used for: Allegiance change (300 notes), Sigil bonuses (50 notes each), Teleportation in uncontrolled areas (variable cost, higher in non-controlled areas).

    9. Campaign Cycle (Weekly Flow):
        a. Tallying Results: Once per week (Monday after Japan midnight), tactical assessment results and Campaign Ops completion rates are tallied to determine battle strategies for the following week.
        b. Determining Policy: Previous week's tally results determine current week's battle strategies and logistics.
        c. Implementing Strategy: Players engage in Campaign battles, Campaign Ops, etc. following predetermined policy. Teleportation services available to active battle areas.
        d. Tactical Assessment: Polling conducted in each nation about implemented military policy.
        e. Steps repeat weekly.
